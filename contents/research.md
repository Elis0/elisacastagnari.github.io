My doctoral work focuses on enhancing **healthcare data interoperability** through a language-first framework. The framework integrates large language models (LLMs), multi-agent orchestration, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to interact with established biomedical ontologies.

This work has deepened my interest in how AI agents coordinate, use tools, and collaborate to address complex problems. I am particularly interested in how architectural choices and orchestration strategies influence the capabilities of these systems.

### Research interests

- Healthcare data interoperability and biomedical ontologies
- Language-first frameworks and large language models
- Multi-agent systems, orchestration, and tool use with MCP

My previous research includes biomedical entity normalization and retrieval, LLM-based anaemia subtype classification, and knowledge-graph approaches to clinical data.
