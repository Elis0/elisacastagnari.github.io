This is where I collect short notes on questions, methods, and lessons from working across AI and biomedicine. I am especially interested in how language models and retrieval systems can make clinical and scientific knowledge more useful without losing sight of evaluation and expert judgment.

### A research question I'm working on

Biomedical entity normalization sounds simple: connect a phrase in a paper to the concept it refers to. In practice, the same concept can appear under different names, languages, or nested mentions, and a retrieval system has to find a useful candidate before a model can disambiguate it.

That problem connects my current PhD work to my earlier CLEF research on multilingual and nested biomedical entity normalization. I am now testing a proposed method and augmented dataset for improving top-1 retrieval accuracy; both are undergoing human evaluation. I hope to use this space to share more about the process as the work develops.
